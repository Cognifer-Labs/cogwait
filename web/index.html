How the give-back works

Fund-OSS mode is on by default: 20% of every dollar Cogwait earns for you is set aside to fund the open source you depend on DEFAULT 20%. Dial it anywhere from 0–100% with npx cogwait --donate <pct> or a slider in the dashboard — lowering it takes one drag, no guilt trip. At payout your balance splits server-side: your share cashes out to you, the give-back share goes to a pooled Cogwait OSS fund.

A local, on-demand scan of your project's package-lock.json builds an honest receipt of which maintainers your give-back represents — nothing about your dependencies ever leaves your machine. Example (illustrative):

Actually private

No code, ever

Prompts, files, and dependency contents never leave your machine. Only a hashed session tag + timestamp reaches the server. Open source — audit every call.

Give-back scan stays local

The dependency scan behind your OSS receipt runs entirely on your machine. Only the give-back dollar total — never a package name or path — ever reaches the server.

Viewable-only

No phantom views

An impression bills only when the sponsor line is really on your screen. No manufactured delay, no background billing.

Zero interference

Runs in the status row with no API-token cost. Network calls are detached with hard timeouts — your terminal never waits.

Keep the rest / set your split

Whatever isn't given back is yours, paid out via Stripe. Set up in 30 seconds:

  1. Configure the status line: npx cogwait
  2. Set your payout id: export COGWAIT_PAYOUT_ID="your-id"
  3. Dial your give-back (optional, default 20%): npx cogwait --donate 10
  4. Restart Claude Code and accept the workspace-trust prompt.

Try it with no backend: COGWAIT_MOCK=1 npx cogwait. Already have a status line? npx cogwait --chain keeps both. Check your setup: npx cogwait --doctor.

Honest about the risk

Whether advertisers pay enough for a one-line terminal placement is unproven — that's the real risk, not the tech. The give-back is a real percentage of real (if modest) earnings, not a guaranteed sum. We won't promise guaranteed income, and we're not affiliated with Anthropic or Claude.
